# AI CANVAS Logo Design Spec

**Date:** 2026-06-06

## Goal

Create a premium brand logo for `AI CANVAS` that visually expresses the idea of an infinite canvas. The result should work as both a standalone icon mark and a full lockup with the `AI CANVAS` wordmark, while fitting the existing dark, restrained, futuristic login-page style.

## Chosen Direction

- Core concept: cropped canvas corners with extension energy
- Meaning: the canvas boundary continues beyond the visible frame
- Priority: icon-first identity, with a supporting wordmark
- Structure: suitable for square app icon usage and standalone brand use

## Visual Principles

- Use a geometric overall silhouette
- Keep the mark restrained, premium, and product-ready
- Avoid generic infinity symbols
- Avoid workflow/node-diagram language
- Avoid decorative complexity that breaks at small sizes

## Final Form

- A near-square primary mark for icon adaptability
- Two to three clear “canvas corner” cues
- One corner carries subtle extension momentum
- Main body uses solid shape language for recognition
- A small amount of line/cut detail adds refinement
- The mark should feel open-ended rather than fully closed

## Brand Tone

- Futuristic but calm
- Elegant rather than flashy
- Product-brand level polish
- Premium SaaS identity, not playful consumer branding

## Color Direction

- Cold silver-blue to muted violet gradient
- Low saturation, controlled contrast
- Must work on deep navy backgrounds
- Should also support a lighter-background adaptation later

## Wordmark Direction

- `AI CANVAS` remains restrained and modern
- The icon mark should dominate over the wordmark
- The wordmark should align with the current login page aesthetic
- No exaggerated italic, loud glow, or overly decorative letter treatment

## Required Deliverables

- Standalone icon mark
- Horizontal lockup: icon + `AI CANVAS`
- Square app-icon adaptation
- Small-size readable version for favicon/app contexts

## Selection Outcome

The chosen concept path is:

- Family: cropped-corner canvas mark
- Refinement branch: concept `C`
- Detail balance: solid-shape dominant with selective fine detail

## Out of Scope

- Full brand system
- Mascot or illustration-based logo
- Animated logo as the primary deliverable
- Multiple unrelated conceptual directions
